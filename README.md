# Awesome-Diagramming-Software



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Flowcharts, Network Diagrams, UML, Whiteboards & Visual Collaboration*

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Diagramming Software**. These tools help developers, architects, and teams create flowcharts, system architecture diagrams, UML models, wireframes, and collaborative whiteboards.



**Examples** include Microsoft Visio, Lucidchart, draw.io, Miro, SmartDraw, Gliffy, OmniGraffle, Creately, Cacoo, and Visual Paradigm (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: The open-source diagramming ecosystem is **exceptionally mature and production-proven**. **draw.io** is the world's most widely used diagramming application with an Apache 2.0 licensed editor, free for everyone, and no artificial scarcity . **Mermaid** enables code-to-diagram creation with simple text syntax and free open-source usage . **Excalidraw** and **tldraw** provide collaborative infinite-canvas whiteboards with self-hosting options . **ChartDB** offers instant ER diagram generation from database schemas .

## 💼 Commercial Tools



> **📊 Market Context**: The diagramming software market is **moderately fragmented** across commercial and open-source solutions. **Lucidchart** starts with a free tier but limits editable documents and objects per document . **Miro** offers a free plan with 3 editable boards and 10 AI credits/month, while Starter is $8/member/month (annual) . **draw.io** is free for up to 10 users on Atlassian Marketplace, with paid tiers from $0.08–$2.02/user/month for larger deployments . **Gliffy** is free for up to 10 Confluence users and $3.80/user/month for 11–100 users . **OmniGraffle** uses a perpetual license model: $149.99 (Standard) or $249.99 (Pro) one-time, or $129.99/year subscription . **Cacoo** introduced a Space Plan at ¥19,800/month with unlimited users and sheets . No single vendor holds a winner-take-all position; teams typically choose based on ecosystem (Atlassian, Microsoft, Google) and collaboration needs.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Diagramming tools handle potentially sensitive system architecture and business process data; ensure proper access controls and compliance with organizational policies.

- **Open-source reality**: The open-source ecosystem for diagramming is **exceptionally mature and production-proven**. **draw.io** is the world's most widely used diagramming application with an Apache 2.0 licensed editor, free for everyone, and no Enterprise tier for SSO . **Mermaid** enables code-to-diagram creation with simple text syntax . **Excalidraw** and **tldraw** provide collaborative infinite-canvas whiteboards with self-hosting options . **ChartDB** offers instant ER diagram generation . However, **commercial tools** (Visio, Lucidchart, Miro) provide **managed infrastructure, enterprise integrations, and polished collaboration features** that open-source alternatives may lack. The open-source path is **genuinely viable** for most diagramming scenarios.
